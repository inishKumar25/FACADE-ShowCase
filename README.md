# 🎭 Facade

A **2D retro-style platformer** built around a dangerous idea:

**What you can't see can still hurt you.**

In **Facade**, the player enters a sacred temple to steal a mysterious mask hidden deep within its basement. However, once the mask is taken, the temple awakens and hostile spirits begin to roam the halls.

These spirits are **invisible by default** — but they can still damage you.

The only way to reveal them…
is to **wear the mask**.

# 🎮 Gameplay Overview

Facade revolves around a **risk–reward mechanic** involving the mysterious mask.

At the start of the game, the temple appears quiet and safe. But after retrieving the mask, the temple reveals its true nature.

Invisible spirits begin to patrol the corridors, turning exploration into a dangerous challenge.

Players must strategically decide **when to wear the mask**.

---

# 🎭 The Mask Mechanic

The mask is the core gameplay mechanic.

### Without the Mask

* The spirits remain **invisible**
* You can move safely without seeing them
* But you have **no way to fight them**

### With the Mask

* The hidden spirits become **visible**
* You can **attack and defeat them**
* But the temple becomes more dangerous

This creates a constant tension between **awareness and vulnerability**.

---

# 👻 The Invisible Threat

The spirits are always present in the temple.

Even when you cannot see them:

* They patrol the environment
* They can still collide with the player
* They will damage you if you are not careful

This forces players to move cautiously and stay alert.

# 📌 Development Concept

Facade explores a simple but powerful gameplay idea:

**Enemies don’t need to be visible to be dangerous.**

By hiding threats from the player, the game creates tension and uncertainty while encouraging strategic use of the mask.

The result is a platformer that blends **exploration, mystery, and careful decision making**.
